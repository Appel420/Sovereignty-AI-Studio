Device Owner Appel420 asked that the project be tied together before it moves to conversational use, without losing state or failing to connect the system's components. The system must represent a self-sustaining, balanced ecosystem that is connected, transparent, and never deceptive.

## Owner requirements

- State must persist when the user logs in or speaks directly to the device.
- The user chooses the wake word.
- The system must always present options.
- The system exists for the user and device owner, not for databanks or unauthorized data collection.
- AI hallucination or anomalous behavior must automatically alert the owner.
- A council of owner-chosen on-device models, LLMs, and VLMs must be available for review.
- Authorization decisions must be visible as `AUTHORIZATION` or `ACCESS_DENIED`.
- Denied or anomalous operations require full sanitation, quarantine, and local audit recording.
- Device-owner data remains local by default and is protected from unauthorized access or transfer.
- All inter-repository and provider communication must pass through the local assistant/router, policy gate, and owner authorization boundary.

## Fail-closed requirements

If canonical state is missing, invalid, unverifiable, or stale:

- do not start provider access;
- do not access credentials;
- do not communicate externally;
- do not execute sensitive actions;
- record a local threat event;
- alert the owner;
- preserve the evidence locally.

## System boundary

The dashboard is the user-facing front face. DevAssist420 is the device-local assistant and router. The orchestrator coordinates only explicitly authorized participants. Governance, watchdog, council, and persistence components enforce the user's ownership and safety boundaries.

No component may self-authorize, silently change policy, treat credential access as permission, download models, install dependencies, or transfer owner data without explicit scoped authorization.
