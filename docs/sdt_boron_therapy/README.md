## What is SDT-Boron Therapy?

**Sonodynamic Boron Therapy (SDT-Boron)** is an experimental cancer treatment modality that combines two established research areas:

1. **Boron Neutron Capture Therapy (BNCT)** — uses ¹⁰B-enriched compounds selectively accumulated in tumor cells; thermal neutrons cause α-particle emission that destroys tumor DNA
2. **Sonodynamic Therapy (SDT)** — uses low-intensity focused ultrasound with sonosensitizer compounds to generate reactive oxygen species (ROS) that trigger apoptosis

**SDT-Boron** proposes replacing the neutron irradiation step of BNCT with focused ultrasound activation of boron-sonosensitizer conjugates — enabling **non-nuclear, non-ionizing** tumor destruction with millimeter precision.

---

## Rationale

Conventional BNCT requires access to a nuclear reactor or particle accelerator, limiting availability to <20 clinical sites worldwide. SDT avoids this infrastructure requirement entirely, using clinical ultrasound equipment.

**Key advantages of SDT-Boron over conventional BNCT:**
- No nuclear reactor required
- Real-time ultrasound imaging guidance during treatment
- Applicable in outpatient clinical settings
- Sub-millimeter treatment precision via focused ultrasound
- Combinable with photodynamic therapy and immunotherapy
