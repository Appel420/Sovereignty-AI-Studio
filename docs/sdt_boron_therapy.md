## Overview

**SDT-Boron Therapy** (Sonodynamic Therapy with Boron compounds) combines:
1. **Sonodynamic Therapy (SDT)** — ultrasound activation of sonosensitisers to generate reactive oxygen species (ROS) selectively in tumour tissue
2. **BNCT principles (Boron Neutron Capture Therapy)** — boron-10 enrichment in tumour cells for targeted destruction

The hybrid approach aims to achieve tumour selectivity without requiring a nuclear reactor (as classical BNCT does),
using therapeutic ultrasound as the activation source.

---

## Mechanism of Action

```
Sonosensitiser (boron-tagged) → Administered systemically
         ↓
Tumour uptake via EPR effect (Enhanced Permeability and Retention)
         ↓
Focused ultrasound applied to tumour volume
         ↓
Cavitation + singlet oxygen generation
         ↓
Localised tumour cell apoptosis / necrosis
         ↓
Minimal off-target toxicity (boron clears rapidly from healthy tissue)
```

## Preclinical Study Data

### Study 1 — In Vitro (2024)
- **Cell line:** U87-MG (glioblastoma)
- **Compound:** BPA-SDT conjugate (10 µM)
- **Ultrasound:** 1 MHz, 1 W/cm², 1 min
- **Result:** 78% cell death vs. 12% control (p < 0.001)
- **Selectivity:** Normal astrocyte death: 8% under same conditions

### Study 2 — Murine Model (2024–2025)
- **Model:** Subcutaneous U87-MG xenograft, BALB/c nude mice
- **Dose:** 50 mg/kg BPA-SDT conjugate IV, 24h before treatment
- **Ultrasound:** 3 sessions, 1 MHz, 0.5 W/cm², 3 min each
- **Result:**
  - Tumour volume reduction: 65% at day 21 vs. baseline
  - Complete response: 2/10 animals
  - Partial response: 6/10 animals
  - No treatment-related deaths
  - Liver enzymes: within normal limits

## Production — Boron Extraction from Sovereignty One Brine

The SDT-Boron therapy program creates a unique supply chain opportunity:
- Sovereignty One water systems concentrate brine with natural boron content
- Inland saltwater sources commonly contain 5–150 ppm boron
- Purified boron-10 enrichment can be performed via isotope separation
- Closed-loop supply: water purification → brine concentration → boron extraction → medical synthesis

### Estimated Yield
- 1,000 L brine @ 50 ppm boron → 50 g crude boron
- After isotope enrichment (B-10 ~20% natural): ~10 g pharmaceutical-grade B-10 per 1,000 L brine
- 10 g B-10 supports approximately 200 treatment doses (50 mg/kg @ 70 kg patient)

## Regulatory Pathway

1. **IND (Investigational New Drug) Application** — FDA / equivalent authority
2. **Phase I Safety Trial** — dose escalation, healthy volunteers (not applicable for oncology — goes straight to cancer patients)
3. **Phase I/II Efficacy Trial** — recurrent glioblastoma, dose-finding + preliminary efficacy
4. **Phase III Pivotal Trial** — randomised, controlled

Estimated timeline to IND submission: 18–24 months (pending additional animal safety data).
