## Overview

Every desalination system produces brine waste. Sovereignty One's Plasma ZLD subsystem eliminates liquid waste while producing **saleable mineral products**. This transforms a cost center (brine disposal) into a revenue stream.

## Plasma ZLD — Mineral Separation Process

### Stage 1: Plasma Treatment
Cold plasma (10 kV, 1–10 kHz) in the quartz reactor:
- Breaks down organic micropollutants
- Destabilizes colloidal suspensions
- Oxidizes Fe²⁺ → Fe(OH)₃ (precipitates)
- Activates crystal nucleation sites

### Stage 2: Electrodialysis Separation (optional upgrade)
Separate monovalent (Na⁺, Cl⁻, K⁺) from divalent (Mg²⁺, Ca²⁺, SO₄²⁻) ions:
- Neosepta CMX/AMX membranes
- ~2 kWh/kg mineral separated

### Stage 3: Fractional Crystallization
Sequential cooling and evaporation:
1. **First cut** (70°C → 50°C): CaSO₄ precipitates
2. **Second cut** (50°C → 25°C): NaCl crystallizes (eutectic)
3. **Third cut** (25°C → 5°C): MgSO₄·7H₂O (Epsom salt)
4. **Residue**: KCl + trace minerals (bittern)

### Stage 4: Packaging
Simple air-drying trays or small spray dryer (200W) produce:
- Coarse sea salt crystals
- Fine powder for industrial use
- Pelletized fertilizer grade (with KCl/MgSO₄)
