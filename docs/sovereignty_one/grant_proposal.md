# Grant Proposal — Sovereignty One Water Systems

---

## Water4All 2026 Joint Transnational Call (JTC) Proposal

**Project Title**: Sovereignty One — Decentralized Solar-Powered CDI/MED/Plasma Hybrid Desalination for Climate-Vulnerable Communities

**Call Reference**: Water4All-2026-JTC-SCOPING

**Requested Funding**: €450,000 (36-month project)

---

### Executive Summary

Sovereignty One is a modular, open-hardware, solar-powered water purification system combining Capacitive Deionization (CDI), Multi-Effect Distillation (MED), and Plasma Zero-Liquid-Discharge (ZLD) into a single autonomous unit. It targets communities in water-stressed regions who face brackish groundwater, seawater intrusion, or industrial contamination — and who cannot afford or access centralized infrastructure.

The system generates no liquid waste, recovers saleable minerals from brine, and is entirely powered by photovoltaics. An ESP32-based controller manages autonomous operation with remote telemetry. Total cost of a community-scale unit is under $6,000 USD.

---

### Problem Statement

- 2.3 billion people lack access to safe drinking water (WHO, 2023)
- Conventional RO desalination requires grid power, RO membranes ($200–$500/unit every 2–3 years), and produces brine waste requiring ocean disposal
- CDI + solar offers 60–80% lower energy cost vs RO for brackish water
- No open-hardware solution exists for remote, off-grid communities to build and maintain their own water system

---

### Technical Approach

**Phase 1 (Months 1–12)**: Laboratory prototype validation
- Build 3 complete Sovereignty One units
- Test against WHO standards for 10 feed water profiles (brackish to seawater)
- Characterize mineral recovery (NaCl, MgSO₄, CaCO₃)

**Phase 2 (Months 13–24)**: Pilot deployments
- Deploy in 2 partner communities (Sub-Saharan Africa / Pacific Islands)
- Train local operators using open-source maintenance curriculum
- Collect 12 months of telemetry data

**Phase 3 (Months 25–36)**: Scaling and open dissemination
- Release all hardware designs, firmware, and BOMs under CERN-OHL-S-2.0
- Publish peer-reviewed performance data
- Establish local manufacturing partnership in pilot regions

## NSF Proposal — Water Innovations for a Resilient Environment (WIRE)

**Program**: NSF 24-578 (WIRE)
**Requested**: $380,000 (24-month)

### Objective

Develop and validate autonomous AI-controlled plasma ZLD for zero-discharge desalination in water-stressed US territories (Puerto Rico, US Virgin Islands, Navajo Nation).

### Innovation

- First integration of cold plasma ZLD with CDI in a solar-autonomous package
- ESP32-AI edge control with local anomaly detection
- Open hardware enabling community self-manufacture

## NASA PSTAR Proposal — ISRU Water for Human Spaceflight

**Program**: NASA PSTAR (Planetary Science and Technology from Analog Research)
**Requested**: $250,000 (18-month)

### Relevance

Sovereignty One's CDI/plasma architecture is directly adaptable for:
- **Mars**: Perchlorate-contaminated regolith water extraction
- **Moon**: Polar ice brine processing
- **Europa/Enceladus**: High-salinity plume water recovery

### Proposed Work

- Adapt CDI electrodes for perchlorate/borate removal at Martian temperatures
- Miniaturize plasma reactor for <50W operation
- Test in Mars-analog environment (Atacama Desert / pressurized chamber)
- Deliver Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 4→5 advancement report
