## Notes

- Carbon cloth grade significantly affects CDI performance; minimum 1500 m²/g BET surface area recommended.
- All wetted parts should be PVDF or PP — avoid metal contact with brine circuits.
- Spare CDI electrode sets (5–10 extra sheets) are recommended for field operation.
- Power supply efficiency: CDI consumes ~0.3–0.6 kWh/m³ at 80% removal efficiency.
